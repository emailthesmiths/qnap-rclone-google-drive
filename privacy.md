# Privacy Policy

## QNAP Google Drive Sync

QNAP Google Drive Sync is a private utility used by its owner to synchronize files from Google Drive to local storage on a QNAP NAS using rclone.

### Google User Data

The application accesses Google Drive data solely for the purpose of synchronizing files authorized by the account owner to local storage.

Google user data accessed by this application is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for any purpose unrelated to the synchronization of files.

### Data Storage

Files retrieved from Google Drive are stored on storage systems controlled by the application owner. The application does not operate a public service or maintain a separate third-party database of Google user data.

### Access

Access to Google Drive is authorized by the Google account owner through Google's OAuth authorization process. The account owner may revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings.

### Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the application owner through the GitHub account associated with this site.
